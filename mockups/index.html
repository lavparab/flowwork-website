Six website directions

Same brand, same copy, six different ideas. Open any one, then use ← → (or the bar at the bottom) to flip between them. Every page works on mobile too.

01Thread

Dark · conversational

The site is a WhatsApp conversation.

The hero plays a real COD confirmation on a phone. You can click through five example chats, and the FAQ is written as a chat too.

Pick this if visitors should see it working within 5 seconds.

02Ledger

Light · numbers-first

"Automation that pays for itself", shown in rupees.

A finance-document look with a live RTO calculator in the hero, a table of where the money leaks, and packages set out like a price list.

Pick this if you sell to founders who decide on ROI.

03Editorial

Light · Swiss grid

Huge type, a strict grid, no decoration.

Built like the numbered sections of your brand sheet. Your tagline is the headline, and the big wordmark closes the page.

Pick this if you want to look like a premium studio, not a tool.

04Control Room

Dark · data

Your WhatsApp as a live operations dashboard.

A live event feed, KPIs, flows drawn as systems, and a deployment timeline that shows the three packages side by side.

Pick this if you want to feel like serious infrastructure.

05Parcel

Light · tactile · India

Shipping labels, stamps and the festival calendar.

An RTO label next to a confirmed one, a packing slip, a live Diwali countdown and packages shown as luggage tags.

Pick this for the most memorable, least "agency" look.

06Loop

Light · calm · premium

The customer lifecycle drawn as one ring.

Your logo's ring becomes the diagram. Scroll through five stages with example chats, and each package shows how much of the loop it covers.

Pick this if you want to sell the full system, not only COD.
